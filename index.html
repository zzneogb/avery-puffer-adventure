💙 Be Gentle & Patient

New puffers need our kindness! 🫶

🛏️ Give new puffers a quiet little room first, so they get strong and healthy
🥰 Pick a happy puffer with a nice round tummy — not a skinny, sad one
🐢 Go slow and gentle. Good things take time!
